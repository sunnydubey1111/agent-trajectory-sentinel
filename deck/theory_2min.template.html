How it is implemented

Five steps on the hot path, none of them a model call

The behavioural path — every step

219 µs, end to end

  • 1 · Encode. Text → numbers by character-trigram hash, ~2 µs. No encoder, no download, deterministic.
  • 2 · Predict. A reservoir of fixed random recurrent weights carries the history; only a ridge readout is fitted. It predicts the next step — the error is the surprise.
  • 3 · Accumulate. CUSUM sums surprise over time, so a slow drift builds up instead of needing one big spike.
  • 4 · Fuse. One detector per channel, alarm on the max — a 4-dim shift is never averaged away into 43.
  • 5 · Threshold. A quantile of healthy validation scores at the false-alarm budget. No labels involved.

The deterministic path — at commit

arithmetic, not statistics

  • Recompute. Rebuild the stated total from the tool results this run received. Nothing to be uncertain about.
  • Cover. Confirm every required lookup was actually made.
  • Contract. Was this tool result ever valid? — reported at the step the bad result arrives.
  • On alarm: roll back to the last checkpoint, tell the agent which check failed — no values — and re-run. Every step after is a fresh model call.
Fitted quantities: a ridge readout, a standardiser, a threshold — 1.7 s total
What the alternatives change: memoryless detectors drop step 3, so slow drift never accumulates — detection 0.37 against our 0.71.  ·  GRU / LSTM replace steps 2–3 with gradient training — same ranking (AUROC 0.82 vs 0.872), 40× slower to fit, and more false alarms on healthy runs (0.113 vs 0.069).  ·  LLM auditors replace the whole path with a 7B forward pass at every step.

Cost — three orders of magnitude below one agent step

Per step, at serving time

what it costs to watch

  • 219 µs median, p95 266 µs, over 4,316 timed steps — the agent step it watches takes about a second.
  • 3.95 MB of state. Fits in a sidecar, a laptop, an edge box.
  • CPU only. No GPU, no second LLM, no fine-tuning, no internet, no model download.
  • Against an LLM auditor at every step, this adds effectively nothing.

To fit, and to build

what it costs to stand up

  • 1.7 s on 240 healthy episodes — against GRU 68 s, LSTM 67 s, TCN 19 s.
  • That ~40× is what makes re-fitting per deployment affordable — and re-fitting is mandatory here.
  • Text → numbers by character-trigram hash. MiniLM, a real neural embedder at 2,200× the cost, adds nothing measurable.
Monitoring costs ~0.02% of what it monitors

Contributions · B

Training — healthy runs only, and no lookahead

  • Learns only from correct, healthy runs. One-class: it never sees a failure, and never needs to. It flags departures from normal — including modes that did not exist when it was fitted.
  • No labelled failures required to deploy. Labels buy +0.014 AUROC (0.812 → 0.826) and cost ≥20 labelled failures per deployment, re-collected whenever the null is.
  • Causal. The score at step t uses x₁..x_t and fit-time quantities only — no future information, by construction.
  • Thresholds from healthy validation only. The labelled split feeds nothing but the isotonic oracle and the escalation operating point; every reported number is test.
Why it matters: a real deployment has logs of runs that mostly worked, and nobody with a spare day to label transcripts. A method needing labelled failures does not get deployed.

Contributions · C

Inputs — observable from outside, and near-disjoint

Three channels

43–60 features total

  • e — meaning / content of the step's output (32 dims)
  • u — uncertainty from the logprob stream (4 dims)
  • m — tool action, latency, length, error flag (7 dims)
  • Near-disjoint, measured: e alone scores 0.988 on looping and 0.000 on goal drift; u alone scores 0.938 on grounding loss and 0.000 on looping.
Multi-channel is a requirement, not a hedge

What that buys

deployability

  • 43–60 numbers replace 4,096–12,288 model internals — so no weights required.
  • Monitors hosted APIs and third-party agents, where internals are never on offer.
  • Logprobs are optional. Ablating the u channel moves AUROC +0.000 — no corpus loses. Works against providers that withhold them.
  • Any agent connects by emitting the Episode format. One adapter, no rewrite.

Architecture — two questions, one gate

The two layers

they ask different things

  • Statistical monitor: "does this run look unusual?" — answerable while the episode unfolds.
  • Deterministic checks: "is the result actually correct?" — does not exist until the agent commits.
  • Checks transfer cold. No retraining, no calibration, nothing to transfer: 110/110 failures caught on llama3.1:8b, 0/10 false positives.
  • tool_contract: 0 false positives in 1,825 healthy episodes; 215 of 218 fire within one step of onset.

Two design results

credit assigned honestly

  • The wrapper generalises. Per-channel max fusion lifts a GRU to 0.76 / 0.873 — past our ESN. The idea is not reservoir-specific, and that makes it the transferable contribution.
  • The judge is optional, and measured. It catches classes the monitor misses (goal drift 21/21) but its real detection is 0.548 against the 0.90 the analysis had assumed.
  • recommended_monitor() picks the right fusion for the telemetry it is handed, so the grounding detector is never dropped.

Robustness & engineering — not one lucky setting

Not a tuning artifact

the anti-cherry-pick test

  • Reservoir size 32 → 256 (8× range): AUROC moves only 0.839–0.853.
  • Spectral radius 0.6 → 1.05, across the cliff the literature warns about: 0.843–0.851.
  • Total movement ~0.01, against a 0.35 detection advantage. No setting is lucky, so none can be cherry-picked.
Deploys without a hyperparameter search

Contributions · F

Outcome — detection closed into task success

  • Task success 52% → 73% over 120 episodes, with detection, rollback and repair in the loop.
  • Zero correct runs broken. The usual hidden cost of any automatic retry, charged and found to be nil.
  • ~1.3 amortised extra model calls per run — the repair fires on 46% of runs, adding ~7.8 s to a flagged one.
  • Retry luck is controlled for. Plain resampling with no guidance recovers 16%; only the margin above that is credited to the repair.
The point: most detection work stops at an AUROC and leaves the benefit to the imagination. This measures whether the agent actually did its job better. It did.

Appendix

Detection closed into an outcome

Every flagged episode rolled back to the same checkpoint and re-run under each rung, paired on the identical prefix and task. n=55 genuinely-wrong episodes, mean of three independent repeats.

The rungs

recovery rate · vs resample

  • none — untouched · 0%
  • resample — rollback + fresh sample · 16% · the control
  • located — + which check failed, no values · 45% · p=0.0005
  • generic — + "re-check your work" · 36% · p=0.035
  • specific — + the finding, with values · 36% · p=0.019
  • recompute · 28% · p=0.17 (n.s.)
  • adaptive · 21% · p=0.61 (n.s.)

What it means

the two findings that matter

  • Retry luck is controlled for. Plain resampling alone recovers 16%; only the margin above that is credited to the repair.
  • Naming the failing check beats handing over the answer. located states no computed value at all and still recovers at least as much as specific — 26 of whose 55 hints contained the correct total outright.
  • Two rungs are reported as failures and kept as comparison arms.
